# Reconstruction of the house price index

The Bank of Albania publishes a Fisher house price index computed by the
stratified median price method over three zones (Tirana; the coastal cities of
Durrës, Vlorë and Sarandë; and the remaining territory), following the Eurostat
*Handbook on Residential Property Price Indices* (2011). The survey covers a
sample of 230 construction firms and estate agents, with response rates between
70 and 81 per cent over the period used.

The published reports state percentage changes, not index levels. Two issues,
for the first and second halves of 2020, additionally state the level relative
to the 2013 base period, which provides the anchor: 40.0 per cent above base
nationally and 43.4 per cent for Tirana in 2020H1.

## Validation

Each chained level implies a year-on-year change that can be compared with the
value reported independently in the corresponding survey issue.

| Period | National derived | National reported | Tirana derived | Tirana reported |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2022H1 | 39.2% | 39.2% | 39.4% | 39.5% |
| 2022H2 | 16.7% | 16.6% | 13.9% | 13.9% |
| 2024H1 | 8.0% | 8.0% | 11.0% | 11.0% |
| 2025H2 | 28.0% | 28.0% | 5.1% | 4.4% |

Maximum discrepancy 0.7 percentage points, consistent with rounding in the
published figures.

## Limitations

The series begins in 2020H1: earlier issues state neither the level against the
base period nor a chain reaching it. Twelve half-yearly observations correspond
to at most 23 usable quarters after linear interpolation, with the first-half
value assigned to the second quarter and the second-half value to the fourth.

The national index is a stratified median and is sensitive to the geographical
composition of reported transactions. In 2025H2 the national index rose 28 per
cent year on year while the Tirana index rose 4.4 per cent, a divergence the
Bank of Albania attributes to coastal price increases. The Tirana index is used
in robustness checks for this reason.
